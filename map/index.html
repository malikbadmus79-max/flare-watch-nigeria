    About this map

    Estimated gas flaring at each of the flare sites in Nigeria in the World Bank GFMR 2026 release, 2012–2025. Use the slider or play button to change the year; click a site for its details.

    All volumes are satellite estimates; operators as recorded; not a statement about any company's conduct; proximity only, not exposure or health.

    Definitions

    • Estimated flaring: satellite-based volume estimate in billion cubic metres (bcm). National values are the sum over all sites.
    • Flaring detected: estimated volume above zero in that year. A volume of zero is shown as "no flaring detected".
    • Persistent site: flaring detected in at least 12 of the 14 years 2012–2025.
    • Operator and field: as recorded in the World Bank 2026 release and applied to all years. "Unattributed" means the release names no operator.
    • Residents within 2 km / 5 km: WorldPop 2025 residents within that distance of the site. Counts are per site and may overlap between neighbouring sites, so they cannot be added up. They describe proximity only.
